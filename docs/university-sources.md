# Official university references — checked 26 October 2026

The expanded session network policy made the official university sites reachable
during final QA. The references below were retrieved and read directly. They
supersede the earlier report that all university sources were blocked.

These are **read-only references**, separate from saved personal requirements.
They do not replace manually entered information, create application deadlines,

## UCL Computer Science BSc

The old programme URL redirects to a course search. The current
[canonical programme page](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/study/prospective-students/undergraduate/courses/computer-science-bsc)
explicitly describes **September 2027 entry / 2027–28**, not 2028.

Verified on that page:

- A-level offer A\*A\*A; A\* in Mathematics or Further Mathematics.
- All 2027/28 applicants must take **TARA** alongside the UCAS application.
  This is not silently substituted with TMUA or SAT.
- English requirement **Level 1**.
- Application through UCAS, code G400.
- Published equal-consideration deadline **13 January 2027, 18:00 UK time**.
- International annual tuition shown for 2027/28 entrants: **£48,600**.

The programme's own international-equivalency selector requests
[this official Germany endpoint](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/study/prospectus/international-equivalency/undergraduate/2027/a_star_a_star_a/DE).
Its response states Abitur **1.4 overall and two subject marks of 14**. It also
maps a required A\* subject to 14 points, an A to 13 and a B to 12, with LK/Advanced
preferred where offered. The app does not infer whether an individual grade
record or semester average proves that final subject requirement.

The [official English requirements page](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/study/prospective-students/undergraduate/how-apply/english-language-requirements)
specifies IELTS Academic **6.5 overall, at least 6.0 in each component** for
Level 1. Other accepted qualifications, result validity and exceptions still
require individual checking.

All these facts remain **provisional for the learner's intended 2028 entry**.
In particular the 2027 deadline is stored as `referenceDeadline`, never as an
actionable 2028 `deadlineDate`. Computer Science facts must not be shown as
requirements for another UCL programme.

## UCLA

The [official international-applicant page](https://admission.ucla.edu/apply/international-applicants)
states that UCLA does not consider SAT/ACT for admission or scholarships and
does not encourage these tests as English-proficiency evidence. It describes
IELTS **7.5 or above as a competitive score**. This is retained as a
recommendation, not mislabelled as a universal minimum.

English-proof applicability depends on the applicant's language of instruction.
The page links to country-specific UC requirements; this release has not
established a fixed German Abitur threshold for UCLA Computer Science 2028.

## UC Berkeley

The [official first-year policies page](https://admissions.berkeley.edu/apply-to-berkeley/first-year-applicants-uc-berkeley/first-year-policy-changes/)
states that Berkeley is **test-free**: SAT/ACT are not used in admissions.
Possible placement or credit use after admission does not create an admissions
requirement. The same source says only invited applicants may send recommendation
letters; unsolicited letters are not accepted.

The [international-applicant requirements](https://admissions.berkeley.edu/apply-to-berkeley/international-students/requirements-for-international-students/)
list IELTS **6.5 minimum** as one possible English-proficiency route. Alternative
proof and exemptions must be checked separately. This general institutional
reference does not establish programme eligibility or a guaranteed admission.

Neither UC reference explicitly confirms a separate 2028 policy. The app retains
the inspection date, source and provisional status.

# Agreed direction

- Native iPhone app is the intended product; browser prototype first for
  testing, because the user currently has no Mac.
- Grade 12, public German Gymnasium; Abitur finishes in summer 2028.
- UCLA and UC Berkeley are goals. Major, entry year, and application year
  are not yet decided.
- Priorities: better grades, English and German, confidence, life structure.
- Each chosen social app has its own 30-minute warning and 60-minute limit.
- After a ten-second pause and reflection, offer a deliberate ten-minute
  access window before limiting the affected apps again.
- Default routine: 60 minutes outside school, editable in the profile.
  This is an assumption while available time remains unspecified.
- No school timetable, current grades, target points, financial plan, or
  preferred major has been supplied. The app does not invent these values.

Next personalization should establish the weekly timetable, current and
target points, study obstacles, preferred activities, and intended university
entry year. Keep the plan small enough to use, and adjust it from real
experience rather than describing any first version as perfect.

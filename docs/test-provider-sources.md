# Test-provider source audit — 26 October 2026

The expanded network policy made College Board and British Council Germany
readable during this release. Earlier blocked-provider notes are superseded
for the specific pages below. This is a dated source snapshot, not a live
booking connection. No test-centre seats, personal registration or payment
has been verified or performed.

## College Board

- [SAT dates and deadlines](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/dates-deadlines):
  confirmed upcoming dates are 7 November and 5 December 2026; 6 March,
  1 May and 5 June 2027. Regular deadlines are respectively 23 October,
  20 November, 19 February, 16 April and 21 May. Late/change deadlines are
  27 October, 24 November, 23 February, 20 April and 25 May. Deadlines use
  11:59 p.m. US Eastern Time. November regular registration is already over.
  Published 2027/28 dates are explicitly **anticipated**, not confirmed.
- [International fees](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/registration/international-testing/fees):
  68 USD registration plus 43 USD international fee, total 111 USD before
  applicable extras. Additional-fee table is labelled through June 2027.
  Certain centres charge 24 USD; late registration is 38 USD. These extras
  are conditional, not silently included in every quoted base fee.
- [Registration](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/registration):
  College Board account; select the actual testing location when registering.
  The directory itself does not prove availability near Wiesbaden/Frankfurt.
- [Identification](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/what-to-bring-do/id-requirements):
  valid original physical photo ID, matching registered name and photo;
  travel to another country normally requires a passport. Full location
  conditions remain the provider's authority.
- [Bluebook download](https://bluebook.collegeboard.org/students/download-bluebook)
  and [device preparation](https://bluebook.collegeboard.org/students/prepare-your-device):
  supported Windows/Mac device, iPad or school-managed Chromebook. iPhone
  is not listed. Start device checks at least 30 days before test day;
  device-loan requests also at least 30 days ahead. Fully charged device
  should support 3–4 hours of operation.
- [Practice](https://bluebook.collegeboard.org/students/practice):
  distinguish unscored previews from full scored practice. The app's brief
  authored diagnostic never produces an official SAT score.

## IELTS in Germany

- [British Council dates, fees and locations](https://www.britishcouncil.de/en/exam/ielts/dates-fees-locations)
  confirms locations including Frankfurt, Heidelberg and Marburg; valid
  identity card/passport at booking and on test day; payment options;
  same-day Listening/Reading/Writing without intervening breaks. Speaking
  and exact venue details are confirmed after booking.
- [British Council IELTS Germany](https://www.britishcouncil.de/en/exam/ielts)
  links the official Germany booking system. Academic versus other test
  variants must match the programme requirement.
- The readable page does **not** expose a reliable current Academic price
  or a concrete bookable date table. Prices and exact availability remain
  unknown and must be checked in the booking system. The app does not
  substitute a remembered fee.
- [IDP Germany](https://ielts.idp.com/germany) returned HTTP 403. It remains
  an official alternative link with unverified local details.

`src/provider-references.js` keeps each fact's URL, verification date,
reference year and status. Global published dates remain separate from
user-entered planned/booked dates and cannot overwrite personal records.
